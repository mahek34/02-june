Q4-Write down the steps (as an algorithm, using simple numbered instructions) for ordering food on Swiggy — from opening the app to completing payment.
Answer:>Algorithm for Ordering Food on Swiggy
1. Start.
2. Open the Swiggy application.
3. Log in to your account.
4. Enter or confirm your delivery location.
5. Search for a restaurant or food item.
6. Select the restaurant.
7. Choose the food items you want to order.
8. Add the selected items to the cart.
9. Review the items in the cart.
10. Proceed to checkout.
11. Select a payment method.
12. Complete the payment process.
13. Receive the order confirmation.
14. End.
